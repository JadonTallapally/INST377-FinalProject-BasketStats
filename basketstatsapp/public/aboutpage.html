About Us!

Welcome and thanks for using BasketApp! At BasketApp we want to provide users with an cluster free data exploring experience. We designed our site to be similar to sites like ESPN and Yahoo Sports, but to be free from constant gambling ads and subscriptions. Our site provides usability in 2 primary areas: game scores and player data. You can check out the "Game Data" page for box scores and quarterly data. Or you can check out the "Stats" page to search for any players stats. You can then use the stats you found to defend your player on twitter! Jokes aside, we hope you enjoy your time on our site. If you would like, you can sign up below to become a member of the BasketStats Community! You can also see all the other BasketStat users! Enter your username and team and hit join. Refresh the page you'll see your name listed!
